# Appendices

=end

The appendices hold human-readable supporting material that explains how a PSS may realise a task without being a task or an operation itself: algorithm and method descriptions, reference papers, and product data sheets.
Their placement and form follow [PSI-MADR] ADR043.
An appendix is informative unless its opening scope statement declares a part normative for the tasks or operations that reference it; those tasks and operations remain the normative entry point.
Every appendix opens with its scope against the TOD body, its origin and licence, and its terminology against the [PSI-TAD].
